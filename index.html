Our Heritage

A Legacy on Glassell Street

Glassell Street has been Orange's antique district for generations. Antique Station carries that tradition forward — a place where history isn't just sold, it's honored.

The Beginning

Antique Station opens its doors on historic Glassell Street, joining a community of dealers who had been serving collectors for decades. The vision: quality over quantity, authenticity over trend.

Building Trust

Word spreads among serious collectors. Our reputation for fair pricing and authenticated pieces brings collectors from across Southern California. Other dealers start referring clients our way.

The Collection Grows

Eight curated categories, 300+ new items monthly. We begin offering authentication services and restoration referrals — not just selling, but serving as a resource for the collecting community.

Today

A fixture on Glassell Street with a 4.5-star Yelp rating and a loyal following of collectors, designers, and history enthusiasts. Still family-operated. Still committed to the same principles: authenticity, fairness, and respect for the past.

Our Promise

Every Piece
Authenticated

In a market flooded with reproductions and misattributions, Antique Station stands apart. We don't guess. We research. Every item that enters our shop is examined, documented, and verified before it reaches our floor.

When we say a piece is Victorian, it's Victorian. When we attribute a piece to a maker, we can show you why. Our authentication process is thorough and transparent — because your trust is worth more than any single sale.

01
Physical Examination

Construction methods, materials, patina, and wear patterns tell the true story.

02
Maker's Marks & Labels

We identify and cross-reference hallmarks, maker's marks, and original labels.

03
Provenance Research

When available, we trace ownership history and provide documentation.

04
Written Guarantee

Major purchases include a written guarantee of authenticity and period.

Our Authentication Guarantee

Every item priced over $500 includes a written authenticity statement. If we've misidentified a piece, we'll take it back — no questions asked. That's not a policy. That's a principle.

Preserving the Past

Restoration & Repair Services

Antiques deserve expert care. We partner with trusted restorers who understand that preservation isn't about making something look new — it's about honoring what's original while ensuring the piece endures for another century.

Furniture Restoration

French polishing, veneer repair, structural stabilization, and finish conservation. Our restorers use period-appropriate techniques and materials.

Clock Repair

From grandfather clocks to mantel pieces, our horologist repairs and services mechanical movements. Your heirloom keeps time again.

Jewelry Repair

Stone replacement, clasp repair, cleaning, and appraisal services. Your vintage jewelry handled by bench-trained professionals.

Art Conservation

Painting cleaning, relining, and frame restoration. Works on paper receive archival treatment. Your art preserved for generations.

Have a piece that needs expert care? Call us at (714) 633-3934 and we'll connect you with the right specialist.
