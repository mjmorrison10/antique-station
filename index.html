What You'll Find

Expertly Curated Antiques
And Vintage Collectibles

Our inventory rotates constantly with new finds arriving every week. From fine furniture to rare collectibles, every piece is selected with care and priced with integrity. You never know what you will find.

Fine Antiques & Furniture

Elegant period furniture, ornate cabinets, and heirloom-quality pieces. From Victorian to Arts and Crafts, our furniture selection is chosen for craftsmanship and character.

Vintage Collectibles

Curated collectibles that tell a story. Rare figurines, limited editions, nostalgic memorabilia, and unique pieces that spark joy and conversation. Always something new to discover.

Antique Art & Paintings

Original oil paintings, watercolors, and prints from accomplished artists. Landscapes, portraits, and abstract works that bring history and beauty to your walls.

Retro Kitchenware

Charming vintage kitchen items from Pyrex to cast iron. Colorful mixing bowls, classic bakeware, and retro gadgets that make cooking fun again. Practical and collectible.

Vintage Toys & Games

Rediscover childhood with vintage toys, board games, and collectible playthings. Tin robots, classic action figures, and rare finds that bring back memories.

Historical Memorabilia

Authentic historical artifacts, documents, and memorabilia. Political buttons, wartime souvenirs, vintage advertisements, and pieces of American history you can hold.

Antique Clocks & Watches

Stunning timepieces from another era. Grandfather clocks, mantel clocks, pocket watches, and wristwatches. Each one inspected, documented, and ready to keep time beautifully.

Estate Sale Finds

Treasures from estate sales across Southern California. Unique items with provenance and character. These one-of-a-kind pieces go fast, so visit often.

About Antique Station

Preserving the Past.
Enriching the Present.

Antique Station has been a fixture on Glassell Street in Orange for years, earning a reputation as the city's premier antique destination. Our passion for history and craftsmanship drives everything we do, from the items we select to the way we price them.

We believe that antiques should be accessible, not just for collectors with deep pockets. That is why we work hard to keep our prices fair while maintaining the highest standards for quality and authenticity. Every item in our shop has been researched and evaluated by our experienced team.

Whether you are furnishing a home, starting a collection, or searching for that perfect gift, our knowledgeable staff is here to help. We love sharing the stories behind our pieces almost as much as we love finding them.
